<p>Shlinker is a native Android client for <a href="https://shlink.io">Shlink</a>, the self-hosted URL shortener.</p>
<ul>
<li>Multiple Shlink instances</li>
<li>API keys encrypted in the Android Keystore</li>
<li>Connection test with clear error messages</li>
<li>Material You, light/dark mode</li>
</ul>
<p>Privacy: no Google Play Services, no tracking, no ads, no cloud backups. HTTPS only.</p>
<p>Shlinker is not an official Shlink project.</p>
